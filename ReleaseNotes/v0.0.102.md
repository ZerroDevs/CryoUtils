# CryoUtils v0.0.102

This release brings the compact **CryoTray-aligned dashboard** anchored directly above your system tray, eliminates first-capture hotkey/tray delay, slashes idle and active RAM usage across clipboard/recent captures/GIF recording, and adds new daily tools including **Zoom Callout (`M`)**, **Auto-Redact**, **Quick Capture Profiles**, **hover Pin HUD**, and **Base64 / Markdown quick copy**.

---

## What's New & Improved

### 1. Compact CryoTray-Aligned Dashboard & Theme Presets
- **Sleek Tray-Anchored Popup (`480×450` Lite / `480×565` Full, `MinWidth=430`):** Replaced the oversized dashboard layout with a compact, resizable `#141414` borderless frame anchored cleanly above the taskbar notification area (`WindowPlacementHelper`) with a smooth `90ms` tray reveal, `Ctrl+K` quick command & library text search (`DashboardSearchService`), and flush underline navigation tabs.
- **Unified `Workspace` Tab (`WorkbenchTabView`):** Consolidated all 8 Workbench panels (`Library`, `Workflows`, `Privacy`, `FileActions`, `Color`, `Media`, `Pinner`, `Diagnostics`) directly inside `MainWindow`, retiring the standalone `PowerToolsWindow`.
- **Accent Color Selector:** Added 6 preset accents (`Cryo Blue`, `Cyan`, `Emerald`, `Violet`, `Amber`, `Rose`) in Settings driving `AccentBrush`, toggle tracks, and hero gradients live across all windows.
- **Lite Quick Strip & Tooltips:** Added 1-click Overview chips in Screenshot-Only (Lite) mode for **Format** (`PNG`/`JPG`/`WEBP`), **Auto-Copy**, **Frame**, **1-Motion**, and **Delay** (`0s`/`3s`/`5s`) with fast localized hover tooltips.
- **Notifications Control:** Added **`Disable all notifications`** (`!NotificationsEnabled`) in Settings alongside toggleable **Run on startup** and **Run in background** (default enabled).

### 2. Zero-Delay Capture, Annotations & Profiles
- **Eliminated First-Capture Delay:** Pre-loaded UI resource dictionaries eagerly, removed DWM window transition animations, switched desktop freezing to zero-copy `BitBlt` into `CreateBitmapSourceFromMemorySection`, and pooled reusable `CaptureOverlayWindow` instances per monitor.
- **Live Window-Hover Snap & 1-Motion Capture:** Hovering before dragging previews Z-order visible windows cleanly (`Space` or single click snaps the window), while **1-Motion** copies immediately on drag release (hold `Ctrl` or `Shift` to open annotation rails).
- **Zoom Callout (`M`) & Inline 4-Color Dots:** Draw 2x circular magnifier lenses directly over UI details (`AnnotationKind.Zoom`, fixed for negative multi-monitor coordinates along with `Blur` and `Spotlight`), and switch between 4 quick preset swatches right on the compact annotation rail.
- **One-Click Auto-Redact:** Scans the selected region via offline Windows OCR word bounds (`RecognizeOcrWordsAsync`) to detect and blur emails, API/JWT/Bearer tokens, cards, and phone numbers with full Undo/Redo support.
- **Quick Capture Profiles:** Cycle non-destructive capture presets (`Default`, `Discord` auto-compress + instant copy, `BugReport` Pretty Frame + cursor coords + `SourceApp · timestamp` bezel stamp, `OcrOnly` instant text extract) from the Hero pill or Tray menu.

### 3. Deeper RAM Cuts, Recorder Polish & Quick Utilities
- **Compressed Clipboard & Recent Captures:** Stored lightweight `<=180px` thumbnails + PNG byte buffers on `ClipItem` and `RecentCapture` (`ResolveFullImage()` decodes on demand), plus disk-spooled GIF recording frames (`RecorderService.FramePaths`) instead of buffering hundreds of megabytes in memory.
- **Idle Resource Reclaim:** Added 90s idle overlay pool expiry with working-set trim (`CaptureController`), 30s inactive heavy tab unloading (`MainWindow`), `CaptureLibraryItem` thumbnail cleanup (`WorkbenchTabView.ReleaseResources()`), and zero-shader `ApertureHaloRing`.
- **Compact Recent Mini-Rows & Quick Copy:** Toggle between cards and compact horizontal mini-rows (`BtnRecentLayout`), view real frame-0 thumbnails for `.mp4` recordings, and copy **file path**, **Markdown** (`![](file:///...)` / inline PNG fallback), or **Base64 Data URI** from the `···` menu or right-click.
- **PinWindow Hover Mini-HUD, OCR & QR Enhancements:** Hover floating pins for `-`/`+` opacity, Ghost toggle, Copy, and Close; pick installed Windows OCR languages (`OcrLanguageTag`); search Full-Suite captures by indexed `OcrText`; copy Wi-Fi passwords in 1 click from `QrResultWindow`; monitor live audio peaks and optional **Keystroke HUD** during recordings.

## Download & Verify

- **Installer — Full Offline (Recommended for zero extra downloads):** Download `CryoUtils-Setup-0.0.102.exe` and verify against `CryoUtils-Setup-0.0.102.exe.sha256`. Installs per-user under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\CryoUtils` without UAC.
- **Installer — Lite (Smallest download, auto-installs .NET 10 if missing):** Download `CryoUtils-Setup-Lite-0.0.102.exe` (`CryoUtils-Setup-Lite-0.0.102.exe.sha256`).
- **Portable Archives:** Download `CryoUtils-0.0.102-win-x64.zip` (Full self-contained) or `CryoUtils-Lite-0.0.102-win-x64.zip` (requires .NET 10 Desktop Runtime x64), verify the matching `.sha256` file, extract, and run `CryoUtils.exe`.
